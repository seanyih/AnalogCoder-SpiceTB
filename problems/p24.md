# Problem 24: A phase-locked loop

*Difficulty: Hard | Type: PLL*

## Task

Design a phase-locked loop.

**Input node name:** `clk_ref`  
**Output node name:** `clk_p`

## Design rules

- Transistor-level design: allowed element types are R, C, L, M (level-1 MOSFET models; you choose the parameters), V and I (DC sources only) and X instances of subcircuits that you define yourself with `.subckt`.
- Controlled/behavioural sources (E, F, G, H, B), switches, XSPICE code models, diodes and BJTs are rejected.
- You may add `.ic` / `.nodeset` cards to help the circuit start up.
- Make sure all the input and output node names above appear in the circuit.
- Supply: 1.0 V on node `vdd` (`VDD vdd 0 1`). The reference clock swings from 0 V to 1 V.
- `clk_ref` is the reference clock input, which the testbench provides. Do not drive it yourself.
- `clk_p` is the PLL output clock, swinging around 0.5 V. It must lock to `clk_ref` with a 1:1 frequency ratio.

## Netlist format

- Submit one complete SPICE netlist in **ngspice** syntax. As usual in SPICE, the
  **first line is the title line and is ignored**; start the file with a `*` comment.
- Node names are case-insensitive; `0` (or `gnd`) is ground.
- MOSFET card: `Mname drain gate source bulk model W=<w> L=<l>`. The terminal order matters.
- Independent sources must be plain DC sources (`Vx n+ n- <value>` or `... dc <value>`).
  AC magnitudes, `PULSE`, `SIN`, `PWL`, ... are not allowed. The testbench applies all
  stimuli itself.
- Analysis and output cards (`.op`, `.ac`, `.dc`, `.tran`, `.print`, `.meas`, ...) are
  ignored, because the testbench runs its own analyses. `.control` blocks, `.option(s)`,
  `.temp` and including any file other than the ones provided for this problem are rejected.
- `.model`, `.param`, `.func`, `.ic`, `.nodeset`, `.global` and `.end` are allowed.

## How the design is evaluated

The testbench runs ngspice on your netlist and checks, in order:

1. The testbench drives `clk_ref` with a 0 V to 1 V clock (30 ns delay, 2% rise/fall time, 48% high time). It replaces any source you put on `clk_ref`.
2. The netlist must simulate without errors. In particular, nodes without a DC path to ground ("floating" nodes) are an error. The input and output nodes must exist.
3. **Lock.** With a 10 MHz reference, a 10 us transient is run. Measured between 8 us and 10 us, `clk_p` must swing at least 0.5 V around its 0.5 V crossings, and its frequency must be within 5% of 10 MHz.
4. **Tracking.** The same must hold with a 12 MHz reference.
5. **Hold-over.** The reference stops after 60 cycles (about 6 us). `clk_p` must keep toggling between 6.2 us and 7.0 us, so the output must come from the loop's own oscillator and not directly from `clk_ref`.
